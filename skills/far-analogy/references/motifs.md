# Motif library (cards; the coach names and approves the minimal structure)
```
MOTIF: | MINIMAL STRUCTURE (no field names): | HOW TO SPOT: | APPEARS IN: | TOOLS: | FALSE FRIENDS: | SOURCE: coach/agent/literature
```
- **Duality**: a hard problem has an easier "shadow" giving bounds. Spot: scarce resources that can be priced. In: optimization (Lagrange), economics (prices), physics (conjugate variables). Tools: Lagrangian relaxation, column generation. False friend: large duality gap.
- **Potential function**: a quantity moving one way at every step forces termination. In: potential games, Lyapunov functions, entropy. False friend: monotone only on a subdomain.
- **Local to global**: understand small pieces, glue under compatibility. In: sheaves, divide and conquer, VRP segment concatenation. False friend: gluing conditions not closed.
- **Multilevel coarsening**: aggregate, solve coarse, refine. In: renormalization, multigrid, multilevel graph partitioning. False friend: blocks lose the key property.
- **Escape local minima by controlled noise**: In: annealing, mutation, SGD noise.
- **Diversity through isolation**: isolated groups keep more directions. In: island models, inefficient networks winning long-run, agent teams.
- **Bottleneck governs the system**: In: theory of constraints, min cut, a football back line.
- **Symmetry breaking**: fix one of many equivalent choices. In: physics, symmetry-breaking constraints in CP, identical vehicles in VRP.
